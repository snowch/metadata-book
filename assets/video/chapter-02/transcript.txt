The page's database records say delivered_at declares TEXT and allows NULL, a missing value. In this SQLite table, the type declaration does not strictly enforce every stored value. What does a missing delivery time mean? Predict an answer before continuing.

The column record cannot settle that. The parcel might be unfinished, the record might be awaiting an update, or the arrival time might be unknown. For this page's complete invented records, NULL means not yet delivered. Structure permits the value; the definition explains its meaning.

Meaning can change without a schema change. A parcel arrives at twelve twenty. Against the original noon promise, it is late. Against a revised twelve thirty promise, it is on time. The column name and type can stay the same. We must know which promise is stored.

The unfinished parcel has the missing time, so the filter removes it. Three completed deliveries remain, including the late one. Two were on time. Two out of three gives 66.7%. The example uses complete records and the page's fixed UTC time format.
