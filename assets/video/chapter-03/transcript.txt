No. Today's data and code may differ. Inspect this run's retained input and calculation. At twelve oh five, two parcels had arrived on time and two were unfinished. All four were due by noon. Assume the invented records are complete.

The retained calculation excludes the unfinished parcels. Two completed deliveries remain, and both were on time. Two out of two gives 100%. The stored input and executed code explain this particular result.

This is how matching records establish provenance for a number. Lineage describes the data's route and dependencies; provenance records a particular dataset or result's history. The terms overlap.
