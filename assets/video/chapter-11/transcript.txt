FAIR means Findable, Accessible, Interoperable and Reusable. Documented retrieval, meaning, provenance and versions support reuse, but FAIR neither requires public access nor grants permission. A planned identifier and license do not establish a completed repository deposit.

Protect the evidence itself: saved queries and samples may reveal individual details. Use parameters and synthetic samples where appropriate, and check indexes and exported copies too. Agree retention by artifact and purpose, including copies. Once the input snapshot is deleted, metadata may explain which input was used without enabling an exact rerun. A deletion record does not prove every unmanaged copy disappeared.
