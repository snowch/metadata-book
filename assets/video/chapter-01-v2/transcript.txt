What the data cannot tell you
Original teaching examples; Kokoro af_heart synthetic narration.

Delivery Operations wants to know: of all parcels due by noon, how many arrived on time?

At one o’clock, two parcels arrived on time, one arrived late, and one is unfinished. All four were due by noon. Assume these records are complete.

First, ask about completed deliveries. The late parcel belongs because it arrived. The unfinished parcel stays outside this group. Two on time out of three completed gives sixty-six point seven percent.

Now answer the operations question. Which parcels belong in its denominator? Pause and explain your choice.

All four were due, including the unfinished parcel. The numerator stays at two. Two out of four gives fifty percent.

Try a separate example with complete records: six parcels due, two on time, two late and two unfinished. What percentage of all parcels due arrived on time? Pause before continuing.

Two out of six gives thirty-three point three percent. Dividing by four would answer the completed-delivery question instead.

Record the population, measurement time and responsible team. Then check the calculation against that definition. A successful query can still answer a different question.
